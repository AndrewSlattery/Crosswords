# Keynesian Cryptics - Homophones

This document outlines the indicators used to take homophones of fodder strings in cryptic crosswords. Throughout, `!ind` denotes the indicator word or phrase, and `!str` denotes the fodder string.


## Verbs

Verb indicators may be used in imperative constructions:

| Construction | Example |
|--------------|---------|
| `!ind !str` | pick up bread = "DOUGH" |

or as past participles:

| Construction | Example |
|--------------|---------|
| `!ind !str` | caught cold = "CHILLY" |
| `!str !ind` | increase reported = "RAISE" |

- broadcast

- deliver
- hear
- make out
- overhear
- pick up
- pronounce
- read aloud

## Settings

Nouns denoting places characterised by speech or audio may be used in various constructions:

| Construction | Example |
|--------------|---------|
| `!ind's !str` | speech's opening = "HOLE" |
| `!str in !ind` | lull in conversation = "PAUSE" |

Other prepositions like `!str on !ind`, `!str via !ind`, `!str over !ind` may also be used.

- conversation
- earshot
- hearing
- mobile
- phone
- radio
- sound
- speech
- telephone

## Interlocutors

These are similar to settings:

| Construction | Example |
|--------------|---------|
| `!ind's !str` | dictator's rule = "REIGN" |
| `!str from !ind` | carp from mouth = "WHINE" |

Though there are overlaps, the set of prepositions used for speakers is different from the set used for listeners.
